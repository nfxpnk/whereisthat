Category: crash, error handling

Problem:
ToUtf8 and ToUtf16 allocate size - 1 without checking whether WideCharToMultiByte or MultiByteToWideChar returned 0.

Why it matters:
Malformed UTF-16/UTF-8 or conversion failure can underflow to a huge allocation and crash.

Suggested fix:
Check size <= 0, return empty/error result, and consider logging GetLastError.
