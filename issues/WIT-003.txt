ID: WIT-003
File and line number: src/app/wit_gui/src/ScanCoordinator.cpp:95-100
Severity: High
Category: UI responsiveness

Problem:
Start() creates the working copy and resolves filesystem paths on the UI thread before launching the worker.

Why it matters:
Large catalogs can make "Add/Update Disk Image" appear hung before the progress dialog even shows.

Suggested fix:
Move working-copy creation into the worker and show progress/cancellable busy state earlier.

Risk of fixing:
Medium

Estimated effort:
Medium

Confidence level:
High
