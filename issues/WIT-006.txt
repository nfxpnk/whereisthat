ID: WIT-006
File and line number: src/modules/wit_scanner/src/FileScanner.cpp:405-561, src/modules/wit_scanner/src/FileScanner.cpp:110-198
Severity: Medium
Category: leak, crash, maintainability

Problem:
FindFirstFileExW handles and libarchive readers are manually closed on many paths. Exceptions from allocation/string conversion/database calls can skip cleanup.

Why it matters:
Scans can leak handles/readers after unexpected exceptions.

Suggested fix:
Use RAII wrappers for FindClose and archive_read_free.

Risk of fixing:
Low

Estimated effort:
Small/Medium

Confidence level:
High
