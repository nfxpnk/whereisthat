ID: WIT-017
File and line number: src/modules/wit_database/src/Database.cpp:108-123, src/modules/wit_database/src/Database.cpp:126-137, src/modules/wit_database/src/CatalogSchema.cpp:20-33
Severity: Medium
Category: leak, error handling, maintainability

Problem:
Some SQLite validation helpers use raw sqlite3_stmt* with manual sqlite3_finalize. Exceptions from string allocation/conversion/comparison between prepare and finalize can skip sqlite3_finalize.

Why it matters:
Leaked prepared statements can keep SQLite resources alive and can make later sqlite3_close fail or leave the database connection in a harder-to-debug state during startup/save validation.

Suggested fix:
Use the existing SQLiteStatement RAII wrapper or a small local statement guard for IntegrityCheckOk, PragmaReturns, and TableHasColumn. Keep return values and validation behavior unchanged.

Risk of fixing:
Low

Estimated effort:
Small

Confidence level:
High
